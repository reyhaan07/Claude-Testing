▣How to use checkpoints to actually learn
Don't just read — retrieve. Cover the answer and force yourself to produce it; the effortful recall is what builds durable memory (this is "active recall," the best-evidenced study technique). Then space it: revisit a part after 1 day, 3 days, and a week. The checkpoints and this list are designed for exactly that loop.
