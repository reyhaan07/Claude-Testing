Part IV

CPU Scheduling

Many processes are Ready; only one CPU is free. Which one runs next? The scheduler answers that question thousands of times a second, and its policy decides whether your system feels snappy or sluggish, fair or starving.
In this part: Why scheduling · The metrics (turnaround, waiting, response, throughput) · Preemptive vs non-preemptive · FCFS · SJF · SRTF · Priority · Round Robin · Multilevel & multilevel-feedback queues · Starvation, aging, the convoy effect · Fully solved numerical problems
