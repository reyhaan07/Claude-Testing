Part XII

Storage Systems

Under the file system lies physical storage — spinning platters or flash chips — each with its own quirks. How the OS orders disk requests, and how RAID combines many disks into one fast or fault-tolerant volume, is the difference between a system that flies and one that crawls or loses data.
