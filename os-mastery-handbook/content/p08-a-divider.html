Part VIII

Virtual Memory

Your programs routinely use more memory than your machine physically has, and it just works. Virtual memory is the illusion — backed by the disk, demand paging, and page replacement — that lets a 16 GB machine run 40 GB of programs.
In this part: What & why virtual memory · Demand paging · Page faults & their handling · Page replacement: FIFO, LRU, Optimal, Clock · Belady's anomaly · Thrashing · Working set & locality · Copy-on-write · Memory-mapped files · Solved page-replacement numericals
